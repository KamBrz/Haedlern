About Copilot across SharePoint

SharePoint is where most organisations keep their institutional knowledge — policies, project documentation, team sites and the intranet itself — and Copilot turns all of it from static storage into something you can ask questions of. Copilot in SharePoint helps authors draft and rewrite pages, build sites faster and keep content fresh, while SharePoint agents give every site a conversational front door: scoped, permission-aware AI assistants that answer questions grounded in the content of a specific site, library or set of files.

This session covers both sides — creating content with Copilot as a site author, and consuming content through summaries, Q&A and agents as an everyday user. It's best suited to intranet owners, communications teams, knowledge managers, site owners and department leads whose teams constantly ask "where does it say…?". It directly addresses the classic SharePoint problem: the answer exists, but nobody can find it.
