About Copilot in Word & Other Apps

Copilot doesn't just live in the headline apps — it surfaces across the entire Microsoft 365 ecosystem, and most users don't realise how many of the tools they already use every day have AI built in. This session covers the full breadth: Word for document drafting, rewriting and summarising; Edge for AI-assisted research and page summarisation; OneDrive and SharePoint for surfacing file content without opening documents; Forms for generating survey questions; and Loop and Whiteboard for AI-powered ideation and collaborative thinking.

It's particularly well suited to teams finishing a Copilot rollout who have covered the core apps and want to get full value from every licensed feature — or to role-specific groups like HR, communications, project managers and L&D teams who rely heavily on writing, research and facilitation tools. The session is practical and fast-paced, designed to give participants one or two high-value techniques per app rather than comprehensive feature tours.
