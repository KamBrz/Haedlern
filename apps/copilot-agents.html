About Copilot Agents & Notebooks

Copilot Agents are custom AI assistants you build on top of Microsoft 365 Copilot to automate specific business processes, answer role-specific questions and connect to your organisation's own data. Unlike the default Copilot experience, agents can be scoped to a team, grounded in specific SharePoint content or line-of-business systems, and triggered inside the apps your people already use. They're ideal for HR helpdesks, IT support triage, onboarding assistants, sales playbook bots, policy lookup tools and any repetitive knowledge-work where the answer "already exists somewhere but no one can find it".

Copilot Notebooks sit alongside agents as the persistent research layer — structured workspaces inside the Copilot app where you aggregate multiple sources (files, meetings, emails, SharePoint sites) and query across all of them together, instead of losing useful output every time a chat ends. Together they address two of the biggest gaps in most Copilot deployments: valuable knowledge locked in fragmented sources, and research that has to be redone from scratch each time. Best suited to knowledge workers, project leads, analysts, HR, operations and Copilot adoption champions who want to push beyond basic prompting.
