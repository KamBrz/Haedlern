About PowerPoint

Microsoft PowerPoint is the standard for business presentations — now enhanced with Designer for automatic layouts, Presenter Coach for rehearsal and Copilot for generating entire decks from a prompt. Modern PowerPoint is far more than bullet lists on slides; it's a storytelling, design and live-presenting platform.

It's best used for leadership updates, sales pitches, internal training, town halls, client-facing proposals and any story that benefits from visual structure. Leadership, sales, marketing, L&D, HR and anyone presenting regularly benefits most. PowerPoint solves the widespread problem of decks that are slow to build, inconsistent in brand, and painful to present — by combining templates, design intelligence and Copilot into a faster, more polished way to build and deliver presentations that actually land.

Getting around

We begin with the PowerPoint interface and its views — Normal, Outline, Slide Sorter and Presenter — so you always know the fastest way to work. Design that stays on brand

Visuals that carry the story

Bringing slides to life with images, icons and stock photos — including removing image backgrounds and applying picture styles for a polished look. We add structure with shapes, SmartArt and tables, and insert and format charts, linking them from Excel where the data already lives.

Motion & interactivity

Using movement with intent: slide transitions with sensible types and timing, then entrance, emphasis, exit and motion-path animations sequenced and controlled through the Animation Pane. We also add hyperlinks and action buttons to build genuinely interactive presentations.

Delivering with confidence
