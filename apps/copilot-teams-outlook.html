About Copilot in Teams & Outlook

For most knowledge workers, Teams and Outlook together account for the majority of their working day — meetings, chats, email and calendar. Copilot embedded inside both apps is where the time savings are most immediately visible: a one-click meeting summary, a catch-me-up on a 200-message thread, a first draft of a reply written in your tone in ten seconds. Unlike prompting from scratch in the Copilot app, these features sit directly in the flow of communication work, requiring no context-switching and minimal prompting skill to get real value.

This session covers both apps together because the workflows overlap: preparing for a meeting, catching up on what happened, following up afterwards and managing the resulting email thread are one continuous flow. It's best suited to anyone who spends a significant portion of their day in meetings or managing communication — managers, executives, project leads, sales teams, HR business partners, executive assistants and customer-facing roles.
