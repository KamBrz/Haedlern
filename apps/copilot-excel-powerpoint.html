About Excel Agent

Excel is where most of an organisation's data work happens — and it's also where Copilot has taken its biggest step forward. Copilot in Excel lets users ask natural-language questions of their data, generate and explain complex formulas, identify trends and create charts without writing a single function. Agent Mode goes further: instead of answering one question at a time, you describe the outcome you need — "build a monthly budget tracker from this export" or "clean this dataset and summarise it by region" — and the agent plans the steps, executes them in the workbook, checks its own results and refines them until the job is done.

This session covers both layers: the everyday Copilot pane for quick analysis, and Agent Mode for multi-step spreadsheet work that would otherwise take hours or specialist skills. Best suited to analysts, finance teams, project managers, operations leads and anyone who lives in spreadsheets — including the many users whose data work is currently limited by what formulas they happen to know.
