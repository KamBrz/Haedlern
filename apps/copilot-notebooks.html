About Copilot Cowork

Copilot Cowork is Microsoft's evolution of AI assistance from answering questions to actually getting work done. Rather than a single prompt and response, Cowork takes a goal you describe — "prepare me for Thursday's client meeting" or "pull together a competitive analysis for the new product launch" — builds a step-by-step plan grounded in your emails, meetings, calendar and files, and then executes it across M365 tools with your approval at each stage. It produces interconnected deliverables: briefing documents, Excel workbooks, PowerPoint decks, resolved calendar conflicts and drafted follow-ups — all within your organisation's security and compliance boundaries. Currently rolling out to early access customers via the Frontier programme, it represents where Copilot is heading and is an important capability for Copilot champions and adoption leads to understand now.

Cowork addresses the biggest gap in most Copilot deployments: people can't get Copilot to do anything more than one simple task at a time. This session builds the skills that agentic work depends on — writing a clear outcome brief, reviewing and approving a plan before it runs, steering a task in progress and auditing what the AI actually did. It's best suited to knowledge workers, project leads, managers, analysts and Copilot adoption champions who want to push beyond basic prompting and understand where Copilot is heading next.
